# Top Applicant Tracking System (ATS) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Recruitment Automation, Candidate Management & Hiring Workflows*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)**. These tools manage job postings, candidate applications, resume parsing, interview scheduling, and hiring pipelines for companies, recruiters, and HR teams.

**Examples** include Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, JazzHR, Breezy HR, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor, and Jobvite (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom hiring workflows, and transparent recruitment data management — ideal for companies, HR teams, and developers building vendor-independent ATS solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/)**  
  Comprehensive hiring platform with structured interviewing, scorecards, and talent analytics for data-driven recruiting teams.

- **[Lever](https://www.lever.co/)**  
  Talent acquisition suite combining ATS and CRM capabilities with candidate relationship management.

- **[Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/)**  
  All-in-one recruiting platform with ATS, scheduling, analytics, and sourcing, designed for high-growth companies.

- **[Workable](https://www.workable.com/)**  
  End-to-end recruiting software with job posting, candidate sourcing, and collaborative hiring tools.

- **[JazzHR](https://www.jazzhr.com/)**  
  Affordable ATS designed for small and mid-sized businesses with easy setup and intuitive workflows.

- **[Breezy HR](https://breezy.hr/)**  
  Visual hiring pipeline software with drag-and-drop candidate management and automated workflows.

- **[Recruitee](https://recruitee.com/)**  
  Collaborative hiring platform with customizable pipelines, careers site builder, and team collaboration features.

- **[SmartRecruiters](https://www.smartrecruiters.com/)**  
  Enterprise-grade talent acquisition platform with marketplace integrations and AI-powered sourcing.

- **[Teamtailor](https://www.teamtailor.com/)**  
  Employer branding and recruiting platform with careers site, candidate management, and team features.

- **[Jobvite](https://www.jobvite.com/)**  
  Talent acquisition suite with ATS, CRM, and onboarding capabilities for growing companies.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OpenCATS](https://github.com/opencats/OpenCATS)**  
  Free and open-source Candidate/Applicant Tracking System designed for recruiters to manage the full recruiting process from job posting through candidate selection and submission. PHP-based with 575+ stars, Docker support available, and active maintenance with PHP 8.4/8.5 modernization roadmap .

- **[SpotAxis](https://github.com/Assystant/SpotAxis)**  
  Open-source (MIT Licensed) ATS for streamlining the hiring process. Features job posting with templates, custom branded career sites, resume parsing, custom application forms, collaborative hiring, custom hiring pipelines, and candidate rating/comparison. Supports two user types: companies managing their own recruitment, and developers/entrepreneurs building SaaS offerings on top of it .

- **[PeelJobs](https://github.com/MicroPyramid/opensource-job-portal)**  
  Dynamic job board platform built with Django 4.2 and PostgreSQL. Implements role-based access control with distinct user types (Recruiters, Company Admins, Job Seekers, Super Admins). Features smart job posting, application management, walk-in events, bulk operations, analytics dashboard, intelligent search with PostgreSQL full-text search, real-time email alerts, and application tracking .

- **[YAWIK](https://github.com/yawik/YAWIK)**  
  Web application that can be used as an ATS or job board. Modular architecture with 131+ stars, designed for flexible recruitment workflows .

- **[OSSAdmiral/Recruit](https://github.com/OSSAdmiral/Recruit)**  
  Self-hosted corporate recruitment system built with Laravel 10.x and FilamentPHP 3.x. PHP-based with 27+ stars and active development .

- **[plutomi](https://github.com/plutomi/plutomi)**  
  Applicant Management at Any Scale, currently work-in-progress. Built in Rust with 61+ stars .

- **[s1-recruitment-system-manager](https://github.com/gautam-patadiya/s1-recruitment-system-manager)**  
  Recruitment system manager / Applicant Tracking System script built for HR and consulting companies. Vue-based with 40+ stars .

- **[oss-ats](https://github.com/tarasyarema/oss-ats)**  
  Simple, open-source ATS platform that can be self-hosted and customized. Features job posting management, candidate tracking, application process workflow, document storage, and authentication. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and Prisma. GNU GPLv3 licensed to ensure modifications remain open .

- **[Hiremate](https://github.com/GURMUKHsMALHOTR4/Hiremate)**  
  Full-stack job recruitment platform built with Next.js, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL. Features for job seekers include job search, resume upload, application tracking, and recruiter chat. For recruiters: job posting, applicant filtering, accept/reject actions, and candidate chat. JWT and role-based authentication .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Jobberbase** — Open-source job board software with 283+ stars .
- **SkillSync** — Helps HR post jobs and applicants apply by uploading resumes, with skill-based shortlisting and automated offer emails .
- **Engineers-Depot** — Full-stack job portal built with JavaScript .
- **superio-job-portal** — MERN stack job board where recruiters post jobs and candidates apply, upload resumes, and track applications .
- **CrewHRM** — WordPress Job Board Plugin for online recruitment .
- **BaseMax/JobMarketplaceDDDPHP** — PHP job marketplace platform connecting companies and developers .

**Frameworks for building custom ATS solutions**: Combine **OpenCATS** for a mature PHP-based ATS core, **SpotAxis** for Django-based recruitment workflows with SaaS multi-tenancy, **PeelJobs** for a modern Django job board with PostgreSQL full-text search, and **oss-ats** for Next.js/TypeScript-based modern stack. For Laravel-based implementations, **OSSAdmiral/Recruit** provides a FilamentPHP admin panel foundation.
